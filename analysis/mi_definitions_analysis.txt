SYNTHESIS:

Based on analysis of 17 papers (10 general MI, 7 GNN-specific MI):

CORE DEFINITION CLUSTERS:

1. REVERSE ENGINEERING APPROACH
   - Neural networks as "black boxes" to be reverse-engineered
   - Goal: Extract human-understandable algorithms/concepts from weights & activations
   - Analogy: Similar to understanding computer programs by examining source code

2. CAUSAL-MECHANISTIC EXPLANATIONS
   - Focus on causal mechanisms linking inputs → internal representations → outputs
   - Methods: Activation patching, causal interventions, ablation studies
   - Distinguished from correlational/attribution methods

3. CIRCUIT DISCOVERY
   - Identify computational subgraphs ("circuits") responsible for specific behaviors
   - Circuit = connected subset of neurons/attention heads performing coherent function
   - Examples: Name mover heads, induction heads, function composition circuits

4. REPRESENTATION ANALYSIS
   - Study what concepts/features are encoded in neural activations
   - Methods: Probing, feature visualization, sparse autoencoders (SAEs)
   - Key phenomena: Polysemanticity, superposition

5. AI SAFETY MOTIVATION
   - Primary driver: Ensure AI systems remain safe/aligned as capabilities grow
   - Applications: Detect deception, understand goal structures, enable model editing
   - Distinctive feature: Strong safety orientation vs. other XAI approaches

KEY DISTINCTIONS FROM OTHER XAI:
- vs. Behavioral XAI: MI examines internals, not just input-output behavior
- vs. Attribution methods: MI seeks causal mechanisms, not feature importance scores
- vs. Concept-based XAI: MI focuses on actual computations, not post-hoc concept labels
